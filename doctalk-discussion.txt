Expert grounded medical advice consistent with the exact inherent patient profile state is essential. Natural patient-doctor conversations often include nuanced and code-mixed language with noise, repetitions, and deviations of context which render the task of understanding the patient's profile difficult. Therefore, real-time conversational datasets capturing the real-world nuances of such doctor-patient real interactions carry immense value in developing models capable of useful medical assistance. 

A growing body of work happening in medical domain... However, existing medical focused datasets are primarily derived from narrative, textual, and online forum question-answers (\cite) which are often not adequately grounded by expert physicians. Some recent works also capture real medical conversations in a doctor-patient dialogure form (\cite). [TODO: find more contrast] While these datasets provide a broader and more reliable view of medical interactions, they often either lack in volume or in sufficient derived datasets for useful downstream medical tasks. Moreover, these datasets are mostly in plain textual form. However, voice data from these real conversations can also be valuable for training voice agents that reliably and correctly provides medical advice [TODO: more search...]. Furthermore, to the best of our knowledge, no such existing work focuses on Bengali, a low-resource language, spoken by ... who represent a culturally diverse and distinct community. Therefore, a dataset capturing real-world conversations in this language with the regional nuances would be extremely valuable.
